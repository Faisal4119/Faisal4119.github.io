BukhariRAG

Personal project

Question answering over 4,036 scanned pages of the Bangla Sahih Bukhari. A Bangla OCR stage and a parser turn the scans into checked hadith records; retrieval mixes multilingual-e5 embeddings in Chroma with BM25, fuses them with Reciprocal Rank Fusion and reranks with a cross-encoder; Qwen3-8B answers in 4-bit. Every answer cites its hadith number and page, and low-confidence questions are declined.

  • Bangla OCR
  • Hybrid retrieval
  • ChromaDB
  • Qwen3-8B
  • Recall@k · MRR eval

Skin Lesion Classifier

Two YOLO11 models read the same skin photo: one names the condition across 27 diseases, the other flags it benign, malignant or non-neoplastic. Trained on 29.6k images from four public datasets, with near-duplicates removed by perceptual hashing and patient-aware splits to stop train–test leakage. 92% top-1 and 0.81 malignant recall on the malignancy test set; 63.6% top-1 across the 27 diseases. A webcam app runs both models at 52 FPS on an RTX 4060 and abstains when unsure.

  • Python
  • YOLO11
  • PyTorch
  • OpenCV
  • Computer Vision
